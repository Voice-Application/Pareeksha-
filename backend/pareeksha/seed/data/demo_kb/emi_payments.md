# EMI payments at Sahay Finance

## Due date and grace period
Your EMI is due on the 5th of every month. If the 5th is a bank holiday, the EMI is collected on the next working day. There is a grace period of 3 days; no late fee is charged if the payment reaches us by the 8th.

## Ways to pay
You can pay through the Sahay app, by UPI to sahayfinance@upi, by net banking, or at any Sahay branch. A payment link sent by SMS is valid for 24 hours. Cash payments above ₹20,000 are not accepted at branches.

## Auto-debit (NACH)
Most loans are repaid by NACH auto-debit from your bank account. To change the bank account, submit a new NACH mandate in the app; the change takes 7 working days to activate. Keep enough balance in the account one day before the due date.

## Late payment and bounce charges
If an EMI is not paid within the grace period, a late payment fee of ₹500 is charged for each missed EMI. If an auto-debit bounces because of low balance, a bounce charge of ₹590 (including GST) is added. Overdue amounts also attract penal interest of 2% per month on the overdue EMI. Late payments are reported to the credit bureaus once an EMI is 30 days past due.
