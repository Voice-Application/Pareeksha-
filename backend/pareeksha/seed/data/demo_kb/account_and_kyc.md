# Account, KYC and statements

## Updating your mobile number or address
To update your registered mobile number, visit a branch with your Aadhaar or passport; online changes are not allowed for security reasons. An address can be updated in the app by uploading a utility bill that is not older than 3 months.

## Statements and certificates
The loan statement and the interest certificate for income tax can be downloaded from the app under Loans > Documents. A printed copy of the statement costs ₹100 per request.

## Customer care
Customer care is available from 9 am to 7 pm, Monday to Saturday, on 1800-000-4455 (toll free). Sahay staff will never ask for your OTP, PIN or CVV.
