# Foreclosure and part-payment

## Part-payment
You can make a part-payment after paying the first 6 EMIs. The minimum part-payment is equal to one EMI, and you can make at most 2 part-payments in a financial year. After a part-payment you can choose to reduce either the EMI amount or the remaining tenure.

## Foreclosure (closing the loan early)
A loan can be foreclosed after 12 EMIs have been paid. Personal loans with a fixed rate carry a foreclosure charge of 4% of the principal outstanding, plus GST. There is no foreclosure charge on floating-rate loans taken by individuals. To get the foreclosure amount, request a foreclosure letter in the app; the quote in the letter is valid for 7 days.

## After closure
The No Objection Certificate (NOC) is emailed within 15 working days of the loan being closed. If the loan was secured by a vehicle, the hypothecation is removed from the RC after you submit the NOC and Form 35 at the RTO.
